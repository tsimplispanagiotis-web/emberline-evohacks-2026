## What the numbers mean

All requests, resources and people are fabricated. Exactly eight initial cases in the bundled fixed demo are individually proposed by the MILP under documented utility and capacity rules. A simulated 75% meal inventory reduction yields seven proposals. An Elena-offline perturbation changes three volunteers while retaining eight proposals. These are synthetic mathematics and workflow tests, **not** achieved rescue impacts.
